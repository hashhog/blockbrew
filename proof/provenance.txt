sha256=4ba0d7c4…, commit 5ccdd59), which is byte-identical to the live
mainnet unit's /proc/<pid>/exe at assembly. Go embeds build metadata;
a different Go, libc, or build path is expected to produce different
bytes. Behavioural re-runs (R1 shim, in-repo R5 tests) are the stronger
check. See REPRODUCIBLE-BUILD.md.

r1/ r2/ r4/ r5/ is not regenerated from outside this repository.
